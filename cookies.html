Cookie Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

No cookie banner is needed on this build

The current EarnBack landing page does not set cookies, write to local storage, embed third-party media, run analytics, display advertising, or perform cross-site tracking.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on a device by a website. Similar technologies include local storage, tracking pixels, and device identifiers.

2. Technologies used now

None. Navigation, animations, the menu, and the early-access message run locally in the browser and do not store an identifier or send information to EarnBack or a third party.

3. Future changes

If strictly necessary technologies are later introduced, this page will explain their purpose and duration. Analytics, advertising, embedded marketing services, or other optional technologies will remain disabled until the visitor has made the required consent choice.

4. Changing your choice

There is currently no stored choice to change. If optional technologies are added, an always-available privacy control will let visitors accept, refuse, or withdraw consent as easily as it was given.

5. Contact

A dedicated controller contact will be published before EarnBack collects information directly from visitors. For more information, read the Privacy Policy.
