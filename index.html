Questions, answered

Before you
start moving.

Does EarnBack block every app?

You choose essential communication, music, and sport apps before starting. The exact available set depends on iOS controls and will always be shown before you commit.

What if my phone restarts?

EarnBack is being designed to recover a compatible challenge within five minutes. Longer or inconsistent gaps are evaluated using multiple signals, never the restart alone.

What happens if GPS is inaccurate?

Distance is filtered using GPS accuracy, motion, pace, continuity, and step data. If the evidence can’t support a fair result, your committed credits are released.

Can I withdraw unused credits?

That is the intended model. It will launch only through an authorized payment partner, with supported countries, processing times, and withdrawal terms stated clearly.

When will EarnBack be available?

EarnBack is currently being prepared for iPhone. Technical testing comes before a public release date.
